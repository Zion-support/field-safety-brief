## What it does

Field Safety Brief turns your site's hazards, weather, permits, and planned tasks into a clear, crew-ready daily safety briefing — in minutes instead of hours of supervisor prep.

## Key features

- **Task-aware briefings** — hazards matched to the day's actual work orders.
- **Weather & site alerts** — wind, heat, lightning, and air-quality thresholds baked in.
- **Multilingual output** — brief every crew member in their own language.
- **Acknowledgement log** — digital sign-off per worker for audit trails.
- **Incident learning loop** — recent near-misses automatically surface in briefs.

## Who it's for

Construction, utilities, energy, and field-services teams running daily toolbox talks across multiple sites.
