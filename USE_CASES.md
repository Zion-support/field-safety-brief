# Use Cases — Field Safety Brief

[Live app](https://ziontechgroup.com/field-safety-brief/) · [Plans](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/plans/) · [Discovery call](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/)

## 1. Construction toolbox talks
A GC with 14 active sites generates site-specific briefs each morning tied to the day's scheduled tasks — prep time drops from 45 min to 5 min per supervisor.

## 2. Utility storm response
During storm restoration, crews receive updated briefs as weather and hazard conditions change, with lightning-stop thresholds built in.

## 3. Multilingual crews
A contractor briefs Portuguese, Spanish, and English-speaking crews with identical, audit-logged content in each language.

## 4. Audit-ready compliance
Digital acknowledgements per worker export directly for insurer and regulator requests.

## 5. Near-miss learning
Incidents logged yesterday appear in today's brief automatically — closing the loop with coaching from [Technician Coach AI](https://ziontechgroup.com/technician-coach-ai/).
